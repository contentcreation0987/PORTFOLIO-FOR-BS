## Non-negotiables for this brand

- Warm paper `#FAF8F5`, ink `#0E0D0B`. Never pure black or white.
- **One accent only** — brass `#A16207` — and only on: result metric, result label, case index numeral, mailto. Never a fifth use.
- Zero corner radii. **No shadows. No cards.** Hairline rules and whitespace separate content.
- Body copy is 15px and quiet; case-study titles are oversized. No mid-scale.
- Motion is one thing: fade + 14px rise, 640ms, once on scroll-in. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no page transitions.
- Labels are the only uppercase type (0.16em tracking). Titles are sentence case at any size.
- **No emoji. No icon libraries. No logo** — set the name in Archivo where a mark would go.
- 9:16 reels: muted, looped, `playsInline`, `preload="none"`, poster frame first, source attached on approach. Mobile-first and fast on slow data.

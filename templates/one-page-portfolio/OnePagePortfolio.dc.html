Movement × Lyari Drop

Client
Movement
Role
Concept, direction, edit
Year
2025

The Chaos Tee — ink, records, the mess you still think about.

Brief

Movement wanted a capsule tied to a place rather than a trend, launched with no paid media.

Concept

Make it read as Lyari-made, not Lyari-themed. The print is a collage of the neighbourhood — the clock tower, the boxing gym, Kakri Ground — so the garment carries its own location.

Execution

Two days on the ground in black and white. Local cast, no studio, no colour grade beyond contrast.

Result

Sold through in nine days, entirely organic.
